### Emerging Trends
The WordPress Core team is focusing on standardizing design controls across the block editor and expanding the capabilities of the Presence API. Gutenberg 24.1 introduces consistent background, color, and typography controls for several core blocks, including Accordion, Tabs, and Comments ([What’s new in Gutenberg 24.1?](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/09/30/whats-new-in-gutenberg-24-1-30-september/)). 

Additionally, the Presence API (v0.14.0) has been updated to support all post types and Site Editor content, while adding a network-wide aggregation system for multisite environments ([Presence API: What’s new](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/10/02/presence-api-whats-new/)).

Regarding release procedures, the team is discussing a transition from live to live-streamed releases during the September 30 Dev Chat ([Dev Chat Agenda – September 30, 2026](https://make.wordpress.org/core/2026/09/30/dev-chat-agenda-sep-30-2026/)).
